# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions. Welcome people with different experience levels. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through their GitHub profile. The maintainer may remove content, close discussions, or limit participation to protect the community. This policy applies to project spaces managed by this repository.
